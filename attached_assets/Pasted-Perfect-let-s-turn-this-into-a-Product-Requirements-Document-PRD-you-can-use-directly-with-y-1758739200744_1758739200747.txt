## 1. Vision & Problem Statement

Access to legal services in Nigeria is fragmented, expensive, and often opaque. Clients struggle to find verified lawyers, and lawyers lack a trusted digital marketplace to acquire clients and manage cases.
This platform will:

* Connect **verified Nigerian lawyers** with clients.
* Use **AI assistants** to help users understand their issues, summarize documents, and draft initial materials.
* Provide **lawyers with AI research and drafting tools**.
* Ensure compliance with Nigerian Bar Association (NBA) and data protection rules.

---

## 2. Goals & Objectives

1. **For Users (Clients):**

   * Easily report a case, upload documents, and get matched with qualified lawyers.
   * Receive AI-assisted summaries and draft documents (with lawyer review).
   * Track case progress, communication, and payments securely.

2. **For Lawyers:**

   * Verified listing with specialization, location, and ratings.
   * Matter management dashboard with secure client messaging.
   * AI-assisted tools for drafting, research, and document summaries.

3. **For Platform (Admin):**

   * Robust lawyer verification (Supreme Court enrollment & NBA practicing certificate).
   * Escrow-based payment system with commission/revenue tracking.
   * Dispute resolution and quality control.

---

## 3. Key Features & Requirements

### 3.1 User Features

* Account creation (email/phone/SSO).
* Guided case intake form + AI triage (case type, urgency).
* Upload documents (PDF, DOCX, images → OCR).
* Lawyer search/matching (specialty, location, price, rating).
* Booking consultations (chat, video, in-person).
* Escrow payment integration (Paystack/Flutterwave).
* Notifications (SMS, email, in-app).

### 3.2 Lawyer Features

* Profile creation & verification (upload call-to-bar certificate, enrollment number, practicing certificate).
* Dashboard for leads, messages, cases, invoices.
* AI-assisted legal research (statutes, precedents, NBA guidelines).
* Document drafting assistant (letters, contracts, pleadings).
* Analytics (number of leads, conversion, revenue).

### 3.3 Admin Features

* Lawyer verification dashboard (manual + auto checks).
* Manage disputes, refunds, and complaints.
* Monitor escrow payments, commissions, and payouts.
* Manage AI logs & compliance auditing.

## 4. AI/ML Components (AWS-native)

### 4.1 Client Intake & Triage

* **Service:** Amazon Bedrock (LLM) or OpenAI via API.
* **Flow:** User fills intake → model classifies into categories (family, property, criminal, corporate, etc.) → generates structured case brief.

### 4.2 Document Summarization & Drafting

* **Service:** Amazon Textract for OCR → Amazon Bedrock/Anthropic Claude for summarization.
* Drafting templates stored in DynamoDB or S3 → LLM tailors clauses.
* Output always tagged as “AI draft – requires lawyer confirmation.”

### 4.3 Legal Research Assistant

* **Corpus:** Nigerian statutes, judgments, NBA guidelines (indexed in OpenSearch or Pinecone).
* **Method:** Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline: user query → semantic search → LLM with context → answer + citations.
* **Service:** Amazon OpenSearch Service + Bedrock.

### 4.4 AI Logging

* Store all AI prompts/outputs in DynamoDB (audit trail for compliance).

## 5. Architecture (AWS Reference)

**Frontend:** React (web), React Native (mobile).
**Backend:** Node.js (NestJS) or Python (FastAPI). Hosted on AWS ECS (Fargate) or Lambda (serverless).
**Database:** Amazon RDS (PostgreSQL) for structured data.
**Vector DB:** OpenSearch w/ kNN plugin or Pinecone (external).
**Storage:** Amazon S3 (encrypted) for documents.
**AI/ML:** Amazon Bedrock for LLM + Amazon Textract for OCR.
**Messaging:** Amazon SNS + SES (notifications).
**Payments:** Paystack/Flutterwave API.
**Auth:** Amazon Cognito (MFA, JWT).
**Monitoring:** CloudWatch, AWS GuardDuty for security.

---

## 6. Compliance & Safeguards

* **Lawyer Verification:** SCN + practicing certificate validation before profile goes live.
* **NBA Rules Compliance:** Follow Rule 39 (advertising restrictions). Lawyer profiles must be factual, not promotional.
* **AI Safeguards:**

  * AI output disclaimers.
  * Lawyer must approve all AI drafts before client delivery.
  * RAG pipeline ensures citations, reduces hallucinations.
* **Data Protection:** NDPR compliance. Consent forms for data storage. Encrypted at rest & in transit.
* **Audit Logs:** All actions logged for dispute resolution.
